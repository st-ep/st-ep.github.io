\section{Research Experience}
\textbf{Graduate Researcher}, UC Berkeley and Texas Robotics at UT Austin \hfill 2024 -- present\par
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{FORM} (lead author): lets a robot identify how an unfamiliar soft material behaves from a single interaction, in seconds instead of minutes, and reuse that physics model to plan shaping and pouring on a real robot.
    \item \textbf{World models} (co-first author): proposed \mbox{query-conditioned} world models, which build the simplest \mbox{physical} model that answers each question about an action; showed that \mbox{vision-language}, \mbox{video-diffusion} and \mbox{action-conditioned} world models fail such questions when hidden physics varies.
    \item \textbf{Neural operators}: lead author of SetONet, a PDE surrogate that works with irregular, incomplete or point-cloud sensor data; sole developer of its open-source PyTorch code. Co-developed probabilistic inverse neural operators.
    \item \textbf{Adaptive control}: sped up Function Encoder neural ODEs about 10$\times$, enabling real-time MPPI control on changing terrain (BeamNG); brought SetONet into meta-reinforcement learning (JAX).
\end{itemize}
